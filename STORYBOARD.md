## Production Notes

### Pre-Production:
1. Location scouting for modern office and home interior
2. Casting for professional character (30s-40s)
3. Smart home app UI design and mockup
4. Equipment preparation (cameras, lighting, props)

### Production:
1. Shoot outdoor scenes during golden hour
2. Film app interface as screen recording or practical phone
3. Shoot home interior scenes with controlled lighting
4. Capture all automated device actions clearly
5. Get multiple takes for each scene

### Post-Production:
1. Edit scenes to 30-45 second total duration
2. Color grade for consistent look
3. Add smooth transitions between scenes
4. Animate app interface if needed
5. Add sound design and music
6. Optional: Add text overlays
7. Final review for clarity and flow

### Quality Checklist:
- [ ] All scenes are clearly visible and understandable
- [ ] App interface is readable and user-friendly
- [ ] Automated actions are obvious and smooth
- [ ] Lighting is consistent and professional
- [ ] Character expressions are genuine and relatable
- [ ] Pacing feels natural (not rushed or too slow)
- [ ] Transitions are smooth
- [ ] Audio enhances rather than distracts
- [ ] Overall mood is positive and comfortable
- [ ] Message is clear: smart home = easy, comfortable living
