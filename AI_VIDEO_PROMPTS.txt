SMART HOME AUTOMATION - AI VIDEO GENERATION PROMPTS
====================================================

These prompts are optimized for AI video generation tools like Runway ML, Pika Labs, 
Stable Video Diffusion, or similar AI video generators. Each scene is designed to be 
generated separately and then edited together.

================================================================================
SCENE 1: OFFICE TO HOME JOURNEY (5-7 seconds)
================================================================================

Prompt:
"Cinematic shot of a modern glass office building at sunset with warm orange and 
purple sky. A tired but relieved 35-year-old professional walks out of the entrance, 
holding a smartphone and work bag. The person checks their phone while walking calmly. 
Golden hour lighting, realistic, professional quality, smooth camera tracking shot 
following the person, 24fps cinematic style."

Camera Movement: Smooth tracking shot, medium wide angle
Lighting: Golden hour, warm sunset tones
Style: Realistic, professional, cinematic

Additional Details:
- Character: Business casual attire, slightly tired expression
- Background: Modern architecture, some pedestrians (optional)
- Mood: Relief, end of workday, transitioning to relaxation

================================================================================
SCENE 2: MOBILE PHONE APP INTERFACE (5-7 seconds)
================================================================================

Prompt:
"Extreme close-up of a smartphone screen showing a modern smart home app interface. 
The screen displays large, colorful, easy-to-understand icons for AC (blue), 
Fan (green), Lights (yellow), and Curtains (purple). Clean, user-friendly design 
with rounded corners and toggle buttons. The app is titled 'Smart Home Control'. 
Bright screen glow, professional UI design, realistic phone screen, crisp and clear."

Camera Movement: Stable close-up shot, slight over-shoulder perspective
Lighting: Screen glow illuminating surroundings
Style: Modern UI, realistic smartphone screen

Additional Details:
- App Design: Minimalist, colorful, easy to read
- Icons: Large and recognizable by children
- Interface: Clean white or light gray background
- Phone: Modern smartphone (iPhone or Android style)

================================================================================
SCENE 3: ONE-TOUCH CONTROL (6-8 seconds)
================================================================================

Prompt:
"Close-up of person's finger tapping on smart home app icons. When AC button is 
tapped, it turns bright blue with cooling wave animation. Fan icon starts spinning 
with rotation animation. Light bulb icon glows yellow with light ray effects. 
Each tap shows smooth, friendly animations. Person's satisfied face visible in 
background. Realistic finger movements, professional UI animations, warm lighting."

Camera Movement: Extreme close-up on phone screen, quick cuts to animations
Lighting: Phone screen bright, face partially illuminated
Style: Smooth animations, realistic touch interactions

Additional Details:
- Animations: Smooth, satisfying, clear visual feedback
- Timing: 1-2 seconds between each tap
- Expression: Satisfied, confident
- Sound Design: Pleasant tap sounds, confirmation beeps

================================================================================
SCENE 4: HOME RESPONDS AUTOMATICALLY (8-10 seconds)
================================================================================

Prompt A (Air Conditioner):
"Cinematic shot of a modern wall-mounted air conditioner unit turning ON automatically 
in an empty room. LED indicators light up blue. Cool air flows visibly with subtle 
mist effect. Modern interior, clean design, professional lighting, realistic mechanical 
action, no people present."

Prompt B (Ceiling Fan):
"Smooth upward tilt shot of a modern ceiling fan starting to spin slowly in an empty 
room, gradually increasing speed. Clean white ceiling, modern fan design, cinematic 
lighting, realistic rotation, professional quality."

Prompt C (Lights Turning ON):
"Wide cinematic shot of a modern living room with lights turning ON automatically 
one by one. First ceiling lights, then table lamps. Warm glow spreads across the 
room. Transformation from dim to bright and inviting. No people, smooth lighting 
transitions, modern interior design, professional cinematography."

Prompt D (Curtains Opening):
"Medium shot of modern curtains automatically opening or adjusting in a contemporary 
home. Smooth motorized movement. Warm natural light entering through window. Clean 
interior, professional quality, realistic automated curtain mechanism."

Camera Movement: Multiple smooth pans, tilts, and wide shots
Lighting: Dramatic transformation from dim to warm and bright
Style: Professional, realistic, automated actions clearly visible

Additional Details:
- Setting: Modern, clean home interior
- Sequence: AC → Fan → Lights → Curtains (1-2 second intervals)
- Mood: Automated, seamless, no human intervention
- Focus: Clear visibility of each device activating

================================================================================
SCENE 5: COMFORTABLE ARRIVAL HOME (6-8 seconds)
================================================================================

Prompt:
"Cinematic shot of a person opening their front door and entering a perfectly

fan spinning, and slight cool mist in the air. Person smiles genuinely, places 
work bag down, walks to a comfortable couch and sits down with a satisfied sigh. 
Relaxed body language, happy expression. Modern interior, warm lighting, professional 
cinematography, realistic human movement."

Camera Movement: Medium tracking shot following person, ending with wide comfort shot
Lighting: Perfect warm interior lighting, inviting atmosphere
Style: Realistic, natural movements, comfortable environment

Additional Details:
- Expression: Genuine happiness, relief, satisfaction
- Actions: Door open → enter → place bag → sit → relax
- Environment: Perfectly prepared, comfortable temperature visible
- Mood: Peace, comfort, happiness

================================================================================

================================================================================

Professional cinematography, slow push-in camera movement, modern interior design,

Lighting: Perfect ambient warm lighting, inviting atmosphere

Additional Details:
- Composition: Phone in foreground, person in background
- Mood: Peace, satisfaction, modern comfort
- Message: Technology enhancing quality of life
- Final Frame: Smart home app visible on phone screen

================================================================================
GENERAL GUIDELINES FOR ALL SCENES
================================================================================

Visual Style:
- Realistic, photorealistic quality
- Professional cinematic lighting
- Smooth camera movements (no shaky cam)
- 24-30fps for cinematic feel
- 16:9 aspect ratio
- Clean, modern aesthetic
- Consistent color grading

Character Consistency:
- Same person throughout (30s-40s professional)
- Consistent clothing (business casual)
- Natural, realistic movements
- Genuine emotions and expressions

Technical Quality:
- High resolution (1080p minimum, 4K preferred)
- Professional color grading
- Consistent lighting throughout
- Smooth transitions between scenes
- Clear visibility of all key elements

Mood and Tone:
- Positive, uplifting
- Easy to understand
- Comfortable, peaceful
- Modern, tech-forward
- Family-friendly, appropriate for all ages

================================================================================
EDITING NOTES
================================================================================

Transitions:
- Use smooth dissolves or cuts between scenes
- Maintain consistent pacing
- Keep total duration 30-45 seconds
- Consider adding subtle background music (uplifting, modern, non-intrusive)

Audio:
- Ambient sounds for each scene
- UI tap/click sounds in Scene 3
- Mechanical sounds (AC, fan) in Scene 4
- Door opening, footsteps in Scene 5
- Peaceful ambient in Scene 6
- Optional: Subtle background music throughout

Text Overlays (Optional):
- Scene 3: "One Touch Control" (3s display)
- Scene 4: "Home Responds Automatically" (3s display)
- Scene 6: "Smart Living Made Simple" (3s display)
- Use clean, modern sans-serif font
- White text with slight shadow for readability

Color Grading:
- Warm tones for indoor scenes
- Golden hour for outdoor scenes
- Consistent skin tones throughout
- Enhance blue for AC, yellow for lights
- Professional, cinema-quality color correction

ALTERNATIVE GENERATION APPROACH
================================================================================

If generating the entire video as one prompt, use this comprehensive version:

"Create a 30-45 second cinematic video showing smart home automation. 
Scene 1: Professional leaving modern office at sunset, checking phone while walking. 
Scene 2: Close-up of smartphone showing smart home app with colorful icons for AC, 
Fan, Lights, Curtains. Scene 3: Person tapping buttons, animations show AC turning 
blue, fan spinning, light glowing yellow. Scene 4: Cut to empty home - AC turns on 
with cool mist, fan starts spinning, lights turn on sequentially, curtains open 
automatically. Scene 5: Person arrives home, smiles at perfectly prepared comfortable 
environment, sits down relaxed. Scene 6: Wide shot of cozy living room with phone 
on table showing app, person relaxed in background. Professional cinematic quality, 
realistic, warm lighting, smooth camera movements, modern interior design, 
happy comfortable mood."
